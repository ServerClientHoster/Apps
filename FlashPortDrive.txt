FlashPort Drive ----------------------------------------

FlashPort Drive was created if you want to store your files nicely in somewhere else
than your files app, and has many features that your files app normally has and even
some other features that make developing and identifying your files easier.

--------------------------------- KEY FEATURES --------------------------
1. Create folders, upload, and of course, download your files.
2. Edit any code or view the code of files you uploaded.
3. Many features good for developing such as size, hex view, size, and many more extra features.
4. Nice auto-sort system to separate your files and search bar to search them.
